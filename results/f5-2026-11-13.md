## Method and limits

- Intervals: sender-clustered bootstrap, 2,000 replicas, seed 20261113, resampling
  `from_hash` clusters (47), not messages.
- Zero counts use one-sided exact Clopper–Pearson (`0/290 → 1.03%`, asserted).
- Raw HTML is judged as source, not as rendered output: `display:none` visibility
  and preheader text are counted as surfaces, never as instructions.
